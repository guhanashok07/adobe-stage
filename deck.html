02 / Core Problem

Creativity Interrupted: The Prompt Wall

Generative AI solved the "blank canvas" problem, but created an even worse bottleneck during the polish phase.

The "Last 10%" Dilemma

A user writes a prompt and gets 90% of a decent design layout. But adjusting a button margin, tweaking a font weight, or nudging an icon requires endless, frustrating text re-prompts.

> "Make the CTA slightly darker blue."
AI regenerates whole layout... destroys previous fonts.
> "No, keep the original layout, just shift the button!"
AI hallucinates new structure. Flow destroyed.

Kahneman's Decision Science

Prompt-only generation forces spatial visual thinkers into System 2 (deliberate, linguistic, exhausting cognitive effort) instead of System 1 (intuitive, direct visual manipulation).

Key Insight: Designers and builders think in spatial relationships, typography hierarchy, and direct interactions—not paragraph syntax.
